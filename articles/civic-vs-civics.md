## A small word with a big fork

On this site, **civic** means government and related employees or institutions.

**Civics** means citizenship — and the work people do, as citizens, on public-good and for-purpose projects.

They are related. They are not the same. Mixing them turns a citizen project into something that sounds like an agency portal.

## What Civics.au is for

Civics.au is owned and operated by Timothy Holborn as part of WebCivics. It gathers citizen-facing proposals and tools: Databox, Walkabout, community grounds, knowledge banks, and the writing that explains them in plain language.

It is **not** a government service. It does not speak as a civic agency.

## Why the distinction matters

When language collapses, people lose the map:

- Citizens become “users” of civic systems they did not authorise.
- Volunteer and cooperative work gets misread as state service delivery.
- Accountability paths blur — who answers for a decision, and under which duty?

Keeping **civics** clear keeps the work honest: citizen-led, reviewable, and built for public good without pretending to be government.
